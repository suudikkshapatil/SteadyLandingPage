About Steady

Sudiksha Patil

Founder & CEO, Steady

Sudiksha is an Electronics & Telecommunication engineering student. During her JEE preparation, anxiety and panic attacks became something she dealt with almost daily — and she couldn't find anything that actually helped in the moment, only things to read about it afterward.

So she built Steady herself. She is a master of JavaScript and C++, with adequate knowledge of HTML, and used those skills to build the entire app end-to-end — from the guided techniques to the mood tracking to the voice guidance.

Using Steady herself has genuinely improved her sleep and reduced her stress and anxiety. She still uses every technique in the app regularly.

Why Steady exists

Most anxiety resources are either expensive, slow to access, or written by people who've never actually had a panic attack at 2 AM. Steady was built to close that gap — real, clinically-established techniques, available instantly, at a price that doesn't require a second thought.
