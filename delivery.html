Delivery Policy

Last updated:

Steady is a digital product. Access is delivered instantly on-screen after successful payment — there is no physical shipping involved.

How delivery works

The moment your payment is confirmed by Razorpay, you're automatically redirected to a confirmation page containing a direct link to open the Steady app, along with download links for all three bonus materials (the 7-Day Calm Reset Tracker, Sleep Without the Spiral, and Panic Button Scripts).

How long delivery takes

Access is immediate — typically within a few seconds of your payment being confirmed. There is no waiting period, no manual approval, and nothing to be shipped or couriered.

If something goes wrong

If your payment succeeded but you weren't redirected to the confirmation page, or a bonus download link doesn't work, please email us with your payment confirmation and we'll send your access details directly within 24 hours.

Access duration

Once delivered, your access to the Steady app and all bonus materials is yours for as long as the app remains available, with no recurring charges.
